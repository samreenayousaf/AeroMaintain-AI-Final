# ✈️ AeroMaintain AI

### AI-Powered Aircraft Maintenance & Inspection Platform

AeroMaintain AI is an intelligent aircraft maintenance platform designed to help aviation maintenance teams monitor aircraft health, perform voice-based inspections, analyze maintenance findings, and generate organized maintenance reports.

The platform combines aircraft fleet management, AI-powered voice inspection, automated information extraction, maintenance analysis, and reporting into a single workflow.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

### ✈️ Aircraft & Fleet Management
- Monitor aircraft across the fleet
- View aircraft health and operational status
- Access aircraft details and component information
- Track maintenance history
- Monitor detected faults and defects

### 🎙️ AI Voice Inspection
- Perform maintenance inspections using voice input
- Capture mechanic observations in real time
- Convert spoken observations into text
- Extract important maintenance information automatically
- Display speech confidence and inspection status

### 🤖 AI-Powered Analysis
- Analyze detected maintenance findings
- Identify potential maintenance issues
- Highlight important risks and defects
- Provide actionable maintenance recommendations
- Help maintenance teams prioritize required actions

### 📊 Dashboard & Analytics
- Fleet health overview
- Maintenance KPIs
- Defect monitoring
- Downtime trends
- Fleet health trends
- Maintenance activity tracking

### 📑 Maintenance Reports
- Inspection summaries
- Maintenance findings
- Aircraft condition information
- Recommended actions
- Maintenance history and reporting

### 👥 Role-Based Access
The platform supports role-based access for different users, allowing features and information to be controlled according to user permissions.
